### Pricing benchmark summary

- Indian SMB salon software clusters at **₹799–₹2,500 per month**. Premium tiers reach ₹3,500.
- Global tools charge **per stylist** ($10–$20 per extra member). For a 6-stylist salon Fresha Team is about $90/month (≈ ₹7,600, estimate at ₹85/USD).
- Zenoti is 10–20x the SMB price and serves chains.

GlowDesk pricing (₹799 / ₹1,499 / ₹2,999 flat per outlet) sits inside the Indian band and well below per-seat global tools. Differentiation is outcome (recovered revenue) and QR queue, not price.

## Trends 2024–2026

1. **WhatsApp is the booking channel.** India has an estimated 600–850 million WhatsApp users; vendors build booking bots on it ([MioSalon blog](https://blog.miosalon.com/how-to-use-business-whatsapp-to-grow-your-salon-and-spa/), [Hyperleap](https://hyperleap.ai/blog/whatsapp-salons-spas-india)).
2. **WhatsApp per-message pricing.** From 1 July 2025 Meta charges per delivered template message. India marketing rate is about ₹0.86 after a roughly 10% rise on 1 January 2026; utility about ₹0.11–₹0.12; service replies within 24 hours are free. Indian accounts must move to INR billing by 31 December 2026 ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing), [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026), [Monty Mobile](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline)).
3. **Tier 2–3 growth and men's grooming.** Rising incomes push chains and franchises into smaller cities; men's grooming named as a growth segment ([IBEF salon landscape](https://www.ibef.org/research/case-study/styling-india-s-growth-the-indian-salon-landscape), [IBEF men's grooming](https://www.ibef.org/blogs/india-men-s-grooming-industry-expanding-product-categories-and-consumer-base)).
4. **Shift to branded networks with memberships.** Nexdigm notes demand moving toward branded networks with stronger memberships ([Nexdigm](https://www.nexdigm.com/market-research/report-store/india-salon-market/)). Independents need membership tools to compete.
5. **Global players dropping free models.** Fresha introduced paid subscriptions in 2025 ([Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/pricing-update)).
6. **AI receptionists.** DINGG and others market AI features ([DINGG blog](https://dingg.app/blogs/how-ai-is-transforming-salon-management-in-2026)). P2 for GlowDesk.
