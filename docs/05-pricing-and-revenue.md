# GlowDesk pricing and revenue

USD equivalents use ₹85 = $1 (estimate). Prices exclude GST (18%, estimate, confirm with CA). Competitor anchors are sourced in [04 market research](04-market-research.md).

## Pricing logic

- Indian salon software sells at ₹799–₹2,500 per month (Invoay, DINGG, MioSalon, Salonist, Zylu). We price inside that band so the owner never feels "expensive".
- Global tools charge per stylist (Fresha $14.95, Booksy +$20, Vagaro +$10 per extra member). We charge **flat per outlet**: a 6-stylist salon pays ₹1,499, not ₹7,600.
- Value anchor: one recovered client visit a week (≈ ₹600) already covers the Growth plan. The sales line is ROI, not features.
- WhatsApp is a pass-through cost with margin, so heavy senders pay more and we never lose money on messages.

## Revenue streams

1. Subscriptions (target 75–80% of revenue).
2. WhatsApp credit margin (10–15%).
3. One-time setup, QR kits and GBP service (5–10%; mostly in first months).
4. Later (P2): referral fees from payment gateway partners, sponsored product-upsell placements from salon product brands (only with clear disclosure), salon-supply ordering.
