# GlowDesk go-to-market and onboarding

## Beachhead

Start in one city where the founder can walk into salons daily. Pick one dense neighbourhood with 40+ salons within 3 km (estimate: typical for a Tier 1–2 residential market). Target order:

1. Men's barbershops and unisex salons with 3–8 chairs and visible weekend queues. Queue pain is obvious, owner is present.
2. Ladies' beauty parlours with package business (facials, waxing, threading on a cycle).
3. Small spas and nail studios (higher ticket, deposit feature sells).

## Getting the first 10 paying customers (weeks 6–12)

| Step | Action | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make a list of 60 salons in the chosen area from Google Maps (filter: 3.8–4.4 rating, 20–200 reviews: growing but not a chain) | 60 names, phones, owner present hours |
| 2 | Visit in person on Tuesday–Thursday, 11:00–13:00 (quiet hours, estimate) | 15 visits a day |
| 3 | 2-minute demo on the owner's phone: scan a sample QR, join queue, receive WhatsApp | 30 demos |
| 4 | Offer the **Founding Salon deal**: Growth plan free for 30 days, then ₹999/month locked for 12 months (33% off), QR kit free, setup done by us. Condition: honest feedback + a video testimonial if it works. | 15 pilots |
| 5 | Install QR standees yourself the same day. Import the client register (photo of the diary is fine; we type it). | 15 live |
| 6 | Day 3 and day 10 check-ins on WhatsApp; day 21 show recovered revenue in person | 12 active |
| 7 | Day 30: convert. Ask for payment by UPI autopay or annual. | 10 paying |
| 8 | Ask each paying salon for 2 owner introductions (salon owners know each other through suppliers and associations) | 20 warm leads |

Founder time: about 4 weeks of full-time field selling. Cash cost: QR kits ≈ ₹5,000, travel ≈ ₹5,000 (estimate).

## Sales script (in-person, 2 minutes)

1. **Open with their pain.** "Sunday mein kitne log wait karke chale jaate hain?" (How many people leave on Sunday after waiting?) Or for parlours: "Aapke kitne regular clients 2–3 mahine se nahi aaye?" (How many regulars have not come for 2–3 months?)
2. **Show, do not tell.** Hand them a QR card. "Scan karo." They join the queue and get a WhatsApp in 5 seconds.
3. **The money line.** "Har client jiska haircut due hai, usko apne aap WhatsApp jaata hai, aur jo wapas aaya uska paisa dashboard pe dikhta hai." (Every client who is due gets a WhatsApp automatically; the rupees from those who come back show on your dashboard.)
4. **Risk reversal.** "30 din free. Agar ₹5,000 se zyada wapas nahi aaya, to mat lena." (30 days free. If it does not bring back more than ₹5,000, do not buy.)
5. **Close on setup.** "Aaj hi QR laga dete hain, 20 minute lagenge." (We install the QR today; it takes 20 minutes.)

## Onboarding checklist (target: live in 1 visit, value in 14 days)

### Day 0 (in-salon, 30–45 minutes)

- [ ] Create outlet: name, address, hours, chairs, GSTIN (optional).
- [ ] Add staff: name, phone, skills, commission rule.
- [ ] Add service menu with prices, durations and cycle days (start from the GlowDesk template for barbershop / unisex / ladies parlour / spa).
- [ ] Connect WhatsApp: salon's own number on WhatsApp Business Platform via our BSP, or start on shared GlowDesk sender while verification completes.
- [ ] Approve message templates (confirmation, reminder, queue position, rebooking nudge, review request) in the salon's language: Hindi, English or regional.
- [ ] Place QR standees: reception + every mirror. Photograph placement.
- [ ] Owner and receptionist do one test walk-in each.
- [ ] Install staff app on each stylist's phone; show commission screen.
- [ ] Import old client list if available (with a one-time consent message, not a marketing blast).
- [ ] Set owner daily summary time.

### Day 1–14

- [ ] Day 1: check first 10 QR joins came through.
- [ ] Day 3: WhatsApp check-in; fix any template or menu issue.
- [ ] Day 7: target 30+ clients captured; if below 15, visit and re-brief receptionist ("ask every client to scan").
- [ ] Day 10: first rebooking nudges start for imported clients who are due.
- [ ] Day 14: owner reviews dashboard with us: clients captured, reviews gained, nudges sent.

### Day 15–30

- [ ] Day 21: recovered revenue review in person.
- [ ] Day 25: offer annual plan with 2 months free.
- [ ] Day 30: convert; set up UPI autopay.

## Activation definition

A salon is **activated** when, within 14 days: QR live at reception and at least half the mirrors, 30+ clients captured with consent, and at least one stylist has opened the commission screen.

## Retention playbook

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| QR joins drop below 50% of previous week | Call receptionist; replace damaged standees; re-train |
| Owner has not opened dashboard in 10 days | Weekly WhatsApp summary is still sent; account manager call |
| Recovered revenue below 2x fee after 60 days | Review cycle days and message copy; add an offer for follow-up nudge |
| Message block rate above 2% | Reduce frequency; rewrite copy; check opt-in flow |
| Stylist turnover | Reassign clients; remind owner that client data stays with the salon |
| Renewal in 30 days (annual) | Send year-in-review: clients captured, visits recovered, reviews gained |

Monthly "GlowDesk report card" WhatsApp to the owner: clients captured, reviews gained, recovered revenue, top stylist. This is the main churn defence.
